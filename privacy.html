Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Nova Media Studio ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Our internal publishing application only uses OAuth access tokens granted by the studio owner to upload and schedule original animated videos to our official TikTok account. We do not collect, store, or sell third-party end-user personal data.

2. How We Use Information

Authorized credentials are stored locally in encrypted environment configurations solely for authenticating requests to the TikTok Content Posting API and internal Telegram approval notifications.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not share any credentials or account metadata with external third parties. Access tokens can be revoked at any time via TikTok account security settings.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Nova Media Studio via GitHub (@gktugkoc).
